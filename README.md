# Stims

The stims from HELLDIVERS 2, adapted to HELLDIVERS. Each Helldiver carries up to 2 stims by default (1 to 4 with the Maximum stim count option) and starts every drop and respawn with a full set. Ammo boxes give 2. Helldivers with full weapons can still pick up an ammo box for the stims.

Press V to stim. Helldivers raise the left fist and stab the injector into the neck, standing, moving or prone. Each stim gives 4 seconds of endless stamina and 60 health per second, up to full health. Knockdowns, reloads, throws and melee attacks cancel the motion before the stim takes effect. Helldivers move at aiming speed during the injection, and the screen edges glow light blue while the stim lasts. The next stim can start once the 4 seconds are over. With no stims left, "Out of stims!" shows over the Helldiver.

By default, you can stim while downed to get back up and heal.

Stims also grant status effect immunity for their duration: no fire, poison, stun, slow or confusion, and any already on are cleared. Slows from water, snow and carried objectives still apply.

Passive health regeneration is off, so health comes back through stims and other healing. The Autoinjector perk keeps the extra regeneration either way.

The loadout card shows a syringe next to the weapon, with one charge per stim. Charges are spent from the needle down. Other players see every stim and the stim count of every Helldiver.

## Requirements

- Obsidian API 1.57.0 or later.
- Every player in the lobby needs the mod, with the same version and options.

## Options

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum stim count | 2 | Stims each Helldiver carries and starts with (1 to 4). |
| Preserve passive healing | Off | Keeps the slow health regeneration of the game. The Autoinjector perk bonus stays either way. |
| Status effect immunity | On | No status effects while a stim lasts, except slows from water, snow and carried objectives. |
| Allow downed stim | On | Downed Helldivers can stim to get back up and heal. |
| Stim sound volume | 0.7 | Volume of the stim sound. Stims of other players nearby play quieter. |
| Use a stim (key) | V | The stim key. |

## Notes

- The stim sound plays through Windows, so the volume settings of the game don't change it.
- Other players see stims about 1 second late.
