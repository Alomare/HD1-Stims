# Changelog

New, changed, fixed or removed features, one line each, brief and direct. The section for a release becomes the release notes.

## 0.1.1

- Changed: A new stim can start only once the four seconds of the last one are over.
- Changed: The three charge syringe on the loadout card is split more evenly.
- Changed: New mod icon.
- Fixed: A stim taken while downed kept healing, and stood the Helldiver up again, when downed again while getting up.
- Fixed: A stim used during a dive no longer shortens the dive.

## 0.1.0

- New: Stims: up to three per Helldiver by default, a full set on each drop and respawn, two more from each ammo box.
- New: Maximum stim count option, from one to four.
- New: A stim key (V by default) raises the left fist and stabs the injector into the neck, then gives four seconds of endless stamina and 60 health a second.
- New: Stims work standing, moving and prone.
- New: Allow downed stim option, on by default: a downed Helldiver can use a stim to get back up and heal.
- New: Passive health regeneration is off, so health comes back through stims and other healing. The Preserve passive healing option keeps it.
- New: The Helldiver moves at aiming speed while the injection plays.
- New: Another stim can start three seconds after the last one took effect, which restarts the four seconds.
- New: The screen edges glow light blue while a stim lasts, and a small spark shows at the stab.
- New: "Out of stims!" shows over the Helldiver when the key finds none left, in the game language.
- New: A syringe on the loadout card shows the stims each player carries, one charge per stim, spent from the needle down.
- New: Other players see each stim and hear it nearby.
- New: Stim sound volume option.
